How We Use Google Analytics
We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a web analytics service provided by Google LLC (“Google”), to collect, track, and analyze how visitors interact with our website. This data helps us improve our website performance, user experience, and marketing campaigns.
Information Collected by GA4
GA4 collects data using cookies, mobile device identifiers, and other tracking technologies. The information generated about your use of the website may include:
• Device and Browser Information: Browser type, device model, operating system, and screen resolution.
• Engagement Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, links clicked, scroll depth, and the website you visited immediately before arriving at ours.
• Approximate Location Data: Derived from your IP address. Please note that GA4 automatically truncates or anonymizes IP addresses by default, and we do not store full IP addresses on Google servers.
Google Signals and Advertising Features (Keep this if you use remarketing/Google Ads; delete if you don't)
We have enabled Google Signals and certain Google Advertising Features. Google Signals compiles multi-platform data from users who have turned on Ads Personalization in their Google accounts. This allows for cross-device reporting, remarketing, and demographics/interests reporting.
State-Specific Disclosures (CCPA / US State Privacy Laws)
For residents of states with comprehensive privacy laws (including California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, and others):
• "Sale" or "Sharing" of Personal Information: Because we use GA4 data to assist with targeted online advertising and remarketing, this practice may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under certain state definitions.
• Your Rights: You have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising. You can exercise this right by clicking our [Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information] link, using a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal in your browser, or adjusting your cookie preferences on our site.
How You Can Opt-Out of GA4 Tracking
You can prevent Google Analytics from recognizing you on return visits to this site by disabling cookies on your browser or using the following methods:
• Install the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
• Adjust your personal ad preferences via your Google My Ad Center Account.

⚙️ Next Steps for US Compliance

To make this legally robust on your actual website, ensure you have completed these technical setup steps:
1. Add an Opt-Out Link: Place a visible footer link titled "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" or "Your Privacy Choices" that connects to a cookie consent banner where users can disable analytical/marketing cookies.
2. Support Global Privacy Control (GPC): Ensure your website's cookie banner is configured to recognize browser-level opt-out signals automatically.
3. Turn on Data Restrictions in Google: If you want to limit how Google uses your data to avoid it being classified as "selling", you can enable Restricted Data Processing in your GA4 admin settings for users in specific states.
